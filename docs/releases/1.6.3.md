# Emotionary 1.6.3 — purchases and Google sign-in hotfix

Build: `2026103102`. Addresses the October 30 team reports.

## Purchases unavailable

The paywall showed "Purchases are temporarily unavailable" because StoreKit returned no products. The app code was unchanged and correct. In App Store Connect, Emotionary Pro Yearly, the Emotionary Pro subscription group, and Emotionary Pro Lifetime were all **Developer Rejected** (removed from review), so 1.6.2 was approved and released without any purchasable products. The Paid Apps agreement, bank account, and tax form were Active.

All three were added back for review in one submission with version 1.6.3, as Apple requires for a first subscription and first non-consumable.

## Google sign-in prompt

Google sign-in opened `ASWebAuthenticationSession`, which showed "Emotionary Wants to Use <project>.supabase.co to Sign In". The session is now ephemeral (`preferEphemeralSession`), which skips that alert. People may need to enter their Google credentials each time.

## Delivery

- 1.6.2's build train was closed (approved), so the version moved to 1.6.3.
- Build `2026103101` was uploaded as TestFlight internal-only and cannot be submitted; `2026103102` is the App Store build.
- 1.6.3 releases automatically after approval, to all users at once.
- 100 Jest tests and TypeScript passed. Not tested on a physical device.
