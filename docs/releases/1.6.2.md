# Emotionary 1.6.2 beta

Build: `2026101401` (app and widget extension).

Implements the October 13 section of [emo app feedback](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1WUqz_SFDgi7x5jfTSJFWfubc2KVa5iFEyRcGf8S91XQ/edit).

- Centers onboarding headings, body copy, reminder controls, widget previews, first-word content, and payment-page introduction. Retains scroll and keyboard access on compact screens.
- Keeps Home Screen widget padding inside its bounds. Shows the word and definition with heart/share controls below; long words scale to fit. All four widget themes remain available.
- Widget heart actions save or remove the displayed word before opening its detail page. Repeated delivery of the same action is idempotent. Share opens that word's share card. Unavailable words do not create orphaned favorites.
- Updates the in-app widget preview to match the simplified content.
- Changes the yearly subtitle to “Billed yearly. Less than your coffee.” and removes the payment-page legal/restore footer and charge paragraph. Restore purchases, privacy, and terms remain in Settings. Verified StoreKit purchases remain required for full access.

## Validation

- 87 tests passed across 16 suites, including new widget save/remove/share and invalid-word regression coverage.
- TypeScript, lint, and whitespace checks passed.
- Native simulator verification, archive, and upload are recorded after completion.
