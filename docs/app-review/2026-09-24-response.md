## Physical-device recording checklist

Use the exact submitted TestFlight build, 1.6.1 (2026092302), on a real iPhone running the latest public iOS. Test on each supported physical device platform before recording. A simulator recording does not satisfy Apple's explicit request.

1. Start recording on the Home Screen, then launch Emotionary. Note the device model and iOS version for the response.
2. Show onboarding, including reminder/widget setup. Notifications are optional; the reminder step has a Not now option.
3. Demonstrate account registration using a disposable demonstration account. If email confirmation is requested, complete it. Show sign-out and a successful sign-in. Test all sign-in options offered by the app before submitting.
4. Show Today, reading a word, pronunciation, saving a favorite, Browse search/category filters, opening a word, Stats/saved words, and generating a share card. Show a working widget if demonstrating that feature.
5. Show the free experience using Continue free. Today's word and the ten-word Browse preview are available without payment.
6. Open Stats → settings icon → Emotionary Pro → Upgrade. Show both Yearly and Lifetime options. Select Yearly and clearly capture the price, one-year duration, automatic renewal disclosure, and working Privacy and Terms links. Show Lifetime and its one-time price.
7. Demonstrate the Apple purchase flow in TestFlight and the unlocked content, and verify Restore purchases. Capture all displayed plans before completing a purchase, because the Upgrade entry changes when access is active.
8. Finish with Settings → Delete account and successful deletion of the disposable demonstration account. Do not delete the separate account Apple will use to review the app. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Do not capture personal passwords, payment details, or unrelated personal notifications. Keep the separate review account active and test its credentials on the submitted build.
